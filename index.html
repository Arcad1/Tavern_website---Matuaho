Our Story

Bj's Tavern was founded in 2000 by Bjorn Johnson, a passionate entrepreneur with a love for great food and drinks. Over the years, our tavern has become a beloved gathering place for locals and visitors alike. We are committed to providing exceptional service and creating memorable experiences for our guests.

Meet my uncle.

My uncle, Bjorn Johnson, is the heart and soul of Bj's Tavern. With his tough personality and dedication to excellence, he has built a loyal following of customers who appreciate his commitment to quality and service. Bjorn is always on hand to greet guests, share stories, and ensure that everyone feels welcome at our tavern.


Our Team

Our team is made up of family members who are passionate about what they do. From our amazing aunties who always cook us a delecious kai, to our friendly bartenders and servers, we work together as a family to ensure that every guest has a fantastic experience at Bj's Tavern. We value teamwork, creativity, and a commitment to excellence in everything we do.
